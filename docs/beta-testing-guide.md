# Try the English learning beta

Open the beta link supplied with your invitation in a recent Chrome or Edge browser. Use a test account you are comfortable deleting. This is an early beta with a small set of everyday situations. It does not certify an English level.

1. **Create your account.** Use an email address you can open. Follow the confirmation email if one arrives. Check spam once. If no email arrives, tell us; do not buy anything or keep requesting messages. Try “Forgot password” once and check whether the reset link lets you choose a new password.
2. **Choose your starting point.** Select your native language, goal and preferred ways to practise. Complete the short assessment honestly. “I don't know,” help and stopping when tired are useful information.
3. **Try your first lesson.** Complete around eight activities. Use help when needed. Notice whether the activity changes after you struggle or succeed. Short, useful replies can be enough in a conversation.
4. **Check voice.** Play an English prompt and confirm that you actually hear it. Allow microphone access only if you want to. Say a short answer and check the displayed words. Try correcting or typing an answer instead. Tell us your device/browser and what happened; do not send private recordings. Browser voice support varies. Typed answers do not prove speaking ability.
5. **Come back for several short sessions.** Try at least three sessions on different days. Look for a mixture of listening, speaking and useful situations, with short practice exercises between them. Notice repeated scenarios and whether review still leaves room for new material.
6. **Change a preference.** In Settings, change a learning preference. During a lesson, reject a method once. The format should change while the learning objective remains useful. Say that something is too difficult, request more speaking, and try the bored/engaged feedback when available. These reports should change the lesson without erasing your progress.
7. **Check My English and history.** Do they reflect what you practised? Is uncertainty clear? One good answer should not imply mastery. A tired day should not undo established ability.
8. **Try installation if offered.** In Chrome or Edge, use the browser's Install option, or on mobile use Add to Home Screen. Close the browser tab and launch the installed app. Check that it opens on its own and that login still works. This is a web app, not an App Store download. If installation is unavailable, report that rather than changing security settings.
9. **Optional account check.** Export your test account in Settings and inspect the downloaded file privately. If finished, delete the test account and confirm that you can no longer sign in. Deletion is permanent. Do not delete an account you want to keep.

## Short feedback form

Use Settings → Send feedback. Do not include passwords, private recordings or sensitive personal details.

- Was the difficulty too easy, about right or too hard? Did it adjust after difficulty?
- Did it repeat the same activity or situation too often?
- Did practice fit your goal, and did the situations feel realistic?
- Did you get enough speaking and enough listening?
- Were explanations useful? Did corrections interrupt too much?
- Did you notice a helpful change of method?
- What was most frustrating? What was most useful?
- Did email, voice and installation work? Include device/browser, not account secrets.

Paid AI voice/evaluation is not required for this beta. If a response cannot be assessed locally, it may remain unscored. There are no live payments or external advertisements in this test.
